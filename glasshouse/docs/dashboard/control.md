# Control

The **Control** tab, `/?tab=control`, turns the TV into a locally controlled device over the home network, operating directly with webOS without relying on LG's cloud services or mobile applications.

## Navigation and playback

![The D-pad opened: arrows around OK, with Back and Home below](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rorygallagher2024/glasshouse/859f5e134e0d9d4b499afe48084b9cdb82db9164/docs/screenshots/control-dpad.png)

* **D-pad navigation:** Directional arrows (Up, Down, Left, Right), OK/Select, Back, Home and Settings (LG's quick settings) to navigate webOS menus and built-in interfaces.
* **Keyboard:** while the Control tab is open, a computer's keyboard works as the remote: the arrow keys, Enter for OK, Esc or Backspace for Back, H for Home, M for Settings, Space for play or pause and K for mute. Keys typed into a field, or pressed with Ctrl, Alt or Cmd, are left to the page.
* **Media playback:** Dedicated transport controls (Play, Pause, Stop, Rewind, Fast Forward) for active media players.
* **Volume and mute:** Volume slider, precision step buttons (`+` and `-`), and a one-click mute toggle.
* **Source selection:** One-click switching across active TV inputs, including HDMI ports and Live TV, with an active indicator marking the current display source.

## Picture and sound

![Picture opened: the picture modes, the OLED light slider and the energy saving choices](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rorygallagher2024/glasshouse/859f5e134e0d9d4b499afe48084b9cdb82db9164/docs/screenshots/control-picture.png)

![Sound opened: the sound outputs, with More for the rest](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rorygallagher2024/glasshouse/859f5e134e0d9d4b499afe48084b9cdb82db9164/docs/screenshots/control-sound.png)

* **Picture mode presets:** Instant switching between calibrated picture modes (such as Cinema, Filmmaker, ISF Expert, Game Optimizer). The presets offered adapt dynamically to what is currently playing, displaying Dolby Vision presets for Dolby Vision content and HDR presets for HDR signals.
* **OLED light and backlight:** Live slider adjusting the panel luminance level for the active picture mode.
* **Energy saving:** Quick toggles for Auto, Off, Minimum, Medium, Maximum, and Screen off energy-saving states.
* **Sound output routing:** Switch audio output between TV Speaker, HDMI ARC, Optical, Headphone, and Bluetooth.

## Panel, timers and power

![Sleep timer opened: Off, 10, 30, 60, 90 and 120 minutes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rorygallagher2024/glasshouse/859f5e134e0d9d4b499afe48084b9cdb82db9164/docs/screenshots/control-sleep.png)

* **Screen blanking:** **Screen off** turns off the OLED or LCD panel immediately while audio and background services continue running. **Screen on** or any remote interaction restores the picture.
* **Screensaver:** Triggers the configured screensaver directly on the TV.
* **Sleep timer:** Sets a timed shutoff (Off, 10, 30, 60, 90, or 120 minutes).
* **Power and reboot:** Switches the TV between standby and on, or performs a full operating system reboot.

## Apps and remote tools

![Apps opened: a button for each app, the one in front marked](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rorygallagher2024/glasshouse/859f5e134e0d9d4b499afe48084b9cdb82db9164/docs/screenshots/control-apps.png)

![Remote tools and utilities opened: a screen notification to send, and a web page to open](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rorygallagher2024/glasshouse/859f5e134e0d9d4b499afe48084b9cdb82db9164/docs/screenshots/control-remote-tools.png)

* **App launcher:** Launch installed webOS applications directly from the dashboard grid.
* **Remote tools & utilities:** Collapsible section grouping quick on-screen actions:
  * **Screen notifications:** Send instant on-screen toast notifications (up to 120 characters) to display messages on the TV.
  * **Open a web page:** Enter any HTTP or HTTPS URL to launch it immediately in the TV's native web browser.
